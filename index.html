Flats and shops for sale in Vangani (W), Thane

Ashtavinayak Complex is a residential and commercial project by Ashtavinayak Enterprises on Pashane Road, Vangani (W), Thane. It has two wings, A and B, each ground plus seven floors, with 1 RK, 1 BHK and 2 BHK flats and shops. Vangani railway station and the Mumbai–Pune Highway are both about 1.5 km away.

The complex has a landscape garden, club house, gym, swimming pool, kids play area, jogging track and parking, and is registered with MahaRERA (PM1270002501104). The booking amount is ₹21,000. See the prices, check live availability, browse the floor plans, or call 93242 49393 to book a site visit, with pickup and drop from Vangani West.
